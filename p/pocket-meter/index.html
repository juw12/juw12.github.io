Where the estimate comes from

What the numbers mean

The expectation is an average across repetitions of these assumptions, not a payout. The ceiling assumes every available prospect buys. The example assumes independent buying decisions, no loss on a missed sale and no shared prospects; overlapping audiences or fixed costs can make the result too optimistic.

Public demo versus the private meter

This page uses two fictional scenarios. It does not connect to a ledger, trading account or payment service. The private tool separately tracks settled earnings, refunds and observed outcomes; none of that data is used here. Edits to this example disappear on reload.
