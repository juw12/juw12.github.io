Why it exists

Saving your feed should not mean a hundred loose files.

Social Saver is one small Windows app for saving posts, reels, videos, stories and highlights from Instagram, Threads, X and TikTok.

Everything for one account lands in one zip, sorted into folders such as posts, reels, stories, highlights and videos.

How it works

From username to zip

  1. 1

    Open it

    Double-click Start Social Saver.bat.

  2. 2

    Sign in if needed

    Press Sign in on a tab for private accounts you follow, stories and most of X.

  3. 3

    Choose

    Type usernames or paste links, tick the types, and set Only the newest if you want a few.

  4. 4

    Download

    Press Download, watch the log, then open the folder.

What's inside

What Social Saver does with an account.

Four platforms, one window

Tabs for Instagram, Threads (beta), X and TikTok. Several accounts at once, one per line.

Pick what to save

Tick posts, reels, stories, highlights or videos per platform, and optionally keep only the newest few of each.

Pick items from one post

Paste a link to a single post and it saves just that one, or press Choose items to see thumbnails and keep only the photos you tick.

One tidy zip per account

Each account gets its own zip with a folder per type, and highlights split by their title.

No duplicates

A download history plus a check of what is already in the zip, so a second run only adds new items.

Sign in on the real page

The platform's own login page opens in a window, or import a cookies file from your own browser. Only session cookies are kept, and Sign out deletes them.

Engines you can update

Built on gallery-dl and yt-dlp. One button updates them when a platform changes its site.

Your data

Your login stays with you

  • You type your password on the platform's own page; the app never sees it.
  • Only the session cookie is stored, on this PC, and Sign out deletes it.
  • No account with us, no server, no API keys.
Good to know

Honest limits.

Straight from the README, so you know what you're getting before you try it.

  • Real signed-in downloads have not been confirmed on every platform yet.
  • Threads support is beta: it pages back through older posts the way the website does, and falls back to the newest posts if Threads changes that.
  • Private accounts work only if your account already follows them.
  • Continue with Google buttons may not work in the sign-in window; import a cookies file instead.
  • Mass downloading can get an account rate-limited, so keep it slow and for personal use.

Questions

Good questions.

What does it run on?

Windows, Python, local.

How do I get it running?

The commands in the “For developers” box above come straight from the README. Paste them into a terminal; the README covers options and troubleshooting.

Where does my data go?

You type your password on the platform's own page; the app never sees it. Only the session cookie is stored, on this PC, and Sign out deletes it.

Will it download the same post twice?

No. It remembers every saved item and also checks the zip, so a rerun only adds new items.

Does it see my password?

No. You sign in on the platform's own page; the app keeps only the session cookie on your PC.

The sign-in window does not work for me. Now what?

Sign in with your normal browser, export the cookies as cookies.txt or JSON with a cookie extension, and use Import cookies file in the app.

What doesn't it do yet?

Real signed-in downloads have not been confirmed on every platform yet. Threads support is beta: it pages back through older posts the way the website does, and falls back to the newest posts if Threads changes that.

Is it free?

Yes. The source is public on GitHub under the MIT licence.
