Privacy notice

Last updated 25 November 2026

Short version: Cairn keeps your notes on your own computer, and I only see what is needed to sell you a licence key.

The Cairn app

  • No account and no telemetry. Your brain (your Markdown notes) stays in your own folder and never leaves your machine unless you push it to git yourself.
  • The app only calls api.github.com and raw.githubusercontent.com when you look someone up or open a published brain.
  • When you enter a Cairn Pro licence key, the app sends the key to the licence server to check that it is valid, and remembers the answer so it also works offline.

Buying Cairn Pro

Payments are handled by Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your payment details and billing information under Paddle's privacy policy. I never see your card number. From Paddle I receive your name, e-mail address, country and order details. I use them only to deliver and support your licence key, handle refunds and meet tax and legal duties. I don't sell or share them, and I don't send marketing e-mail.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts; those services may log your IP address as part of serving the page. The site sets no cookies and has no analytics of its own.
