Job Digest - Privacy Policy

Job Digest is a personal automation used only by its owner, Juwen Lie (juwpegatron@gmail.com). It is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

  • Send email (gmail.send): used to send the owner's job applications, follow-ups, and a daily summary to the owner.
  • Read email (gmail.readonly): used only to check whether a recruiter has replied, so no unnecessary follow-up is sent.

How data is used and stored

Email content is not stored, sold, shared, or used for advertising. Only the recipient address, date and job title of each application are kept in the owner's private tracker. Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
